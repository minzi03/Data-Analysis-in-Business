## Lab Descriptions

### 🔹 Lab 1 – Descriptive Statistics
- **Files:**
  - `Lab1.docx` – Report and instructions.

### 🔹 Lab 2 – Probability & Inference
- **Files:**
  - `Lab2.docx` – Report and instructions.
- **Objectives:**
  - Explore probability distributions (normal, binomial, etc.).
  - Perform hypothesis testing and confidence interval estimation.
  - Strengthen understanding of uncertainty in business decision-making.

---

## Learning Outcomes

By completing all labs, students will:  
- Gain hands-on experience with **statistical methods** and **business data analysis**.  
- Be able to preprocess, analyze, and visualize datasets effectively.  
- Develop skills to transition from **descriptive analysis** to **predictive modeling**.  
- Build a strong foundation for the final research project using real-world data.
